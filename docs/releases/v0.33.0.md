## Changes

- Sources now show separate playback evidence for Live, Movies, and Series, including the checked time, sample attempts, and failure reasons. Background checks run while playback is idle, try up to three samples per category, and stop after a successful sample. Normal viewing can also update evidence.
- Source results are available in the playlist picker. The Sources page has aligned result columns, a blue Details control, a live status area below the playback filters, and playlist actions available from the overflow menu or right-click.
- Movie and series metadata use a compact inspector with Overview, Media info, and searchable All fields. Structured values expand on demand.
- The TMDB / Provider selector switches displayed metadata while preserving the original provider response. TMDB mode fills missing editorial fields from the provider; media and playback information remain provider-supplied.
- Playback recovery and activity tracking improvements are included.

## Requirements and limitations

- Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 26 or later.
- The app uses ad-hoc signing and is not notarized.
- A verified sample proves that sample played when checked. Other channels, movies, or episodes from the same source may still fail.
- Account expiry and connection counts are reported by the provider and may be incomplete or stale.
- TMDB enrichment is best-effort. Provider metadata remains available when TMDB cannot be loaded.
- This release's local verification covers automated regressions, production compilation, and the changed Sources/metadata UI in development Electron. Packaged-app upgrade/restart, live-provider recovery, long-running native-memory behavior, and broad format compatibility were not reverified for this candidate. Passing automated checks is not a guarantee of provider availability or universal playback compatibility.
